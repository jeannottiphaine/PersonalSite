EKO's questions

Raising Awareness About Degrowth

Context & Objectives

The "EKO's questions" book was designed during Hackathon Reboot! 2023, an event dedicated to creating responsible and innovative communication solutions. The central theme of the Hackathon was 'Slow Down or Perish'—a call for collective awareness of the need to rethink how we consume, produce, and live.

Strategy & Insight

Faced with this challenge, we chose to focus on raising awareness about degrowth and responsible consumption among younger generations. The idea was to find a way to address these essential topics in an accessible way while remaining faithful to the Hackathon’s spirit. Degrowth and responsible consumption are crucial concepts for the future, but communicating them can be complex, especially to a young audience.

Creative Solution

One of the main challenges in raising awareness on these issues is that younger generations often have a limited view of how their daily consumption impacts the planet. It seemed essential to us to address these issues in a creative and playful way, without sounding preachy, to create a tool that is both educational and engaging. We therefore chose to create a children's book—an age-old but timeless format—allowing kids to explore these concepts in a family setting, with their parents, who are often the first readers of such content.
